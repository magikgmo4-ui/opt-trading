# Master Target — DCA on Fear, Solid Stocks

## Vision

Accumulate quality equities during market fear episodes using systematic DCA.

## Core principles

1. **Solid stocks only** — blue chips, strong balance sheets, moat, predictable cash flows
2. **Fear entry** — enter only when market prices in fear (VIX elevated, drawdown > threshold, sentiment extreme)
3. **Systematic DCA** — fixed intervals, no timing, no emotion
4. **Hold through cycles** — exit only on fundamentals degradation, not price
5. **Benchmark** — vs buy-and-hold SPY, max drawdown, Sharpe ratio

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| NYSE/NASDAQ liquid equities | Options, futures, crypto |
| Market cap > $10B | Penny stocks, SPACs |
| Positive FCF, investment grade | Leveraged ETFs |
| DCA on fear signal | Active trading, market timing |
| Backtest on 2008, 2020, 2022 | Real capital deployment V1 |
