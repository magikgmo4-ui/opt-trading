# Classification — Solid vs Speculative

## Solid stock criteria (all required)

| Criterion | Threshold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Market cap | > $10B | Screener |
| Revenue growth | > 0% YoY (5yr avg) | Financials |
| FCF positive | Last 5 years | Cash flow statement |
| Net debt / EBITDA | < 3x | Balance sheet |
| Dividend history | > 10 years uninterrupted | Dividend record |
| Sector | Defensive or secular growth | GICS sector |
| Beta | < 1.2 vs SPY (5yr) | Market data |

## Candidate sectors

| Sector | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Healthcare | Inelastic demand |
| Consumer staples | Recession resilient |
| Technology (large cap) | Secular growth, moat |
| Industrials (quality) | Infrastructure spend |
| Financials (large cap) | Too-big-to-fail, regulated |

## Excluded

- Biotech (binary outcomes)
- Energy (commodity cycle dependent)
- Real estate (rate sensitivity)
- Small caps (< $10B)
