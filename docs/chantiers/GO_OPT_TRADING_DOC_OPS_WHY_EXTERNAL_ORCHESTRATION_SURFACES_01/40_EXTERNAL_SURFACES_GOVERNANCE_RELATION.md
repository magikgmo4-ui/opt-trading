# 40_EXTERNAL_SURFACES_GOVERNANCE_RELATION

## Objectif

Relier les surfaces externes candidates a la governance WHY/runtime.

## Relations candidates

| Surface | Relation governance |
| --- | --- |
| ClickUp | suivi et priorisation |
| Botpress | orchestration conversationnelle |
| Knowledge Graph | coherence et relations |
| Airtable | structuration operations |

## Impacts WHY possibles

| Surface | Impact potentiel |
| --- | --- |

## Regles

- Les surfaces externes doivent rester contextualisees.
- Les surfaces critiques doivent etre auditables.
- Les dependances runtime doivent etre explicites.
- Les interactions multi-machine doivent etre documentees.

## Invariant

La governance WHY doit rester prioritaire sur toute orchestration externe.
